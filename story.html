Street collection · No. 01

After Rain

Studio A · Bangkok · 2025

The city becomes quieter just after the rain. Reflections stretch across the road, signs soften, and ordinary corners begin to feel cinematic.

This photograph is part of an ongoing study of passing moments—scenes that may last only a few seconds before traffic, light, and people rearrange them.

“A familiar street can become a completely new place when the light changes.”

The image was composed to keep that temporary stillness intact: a pause between one movement and the next.
